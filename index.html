AI Deal Hunter

A private, single-user research tool that runs on its owner's own computer.

What it is

AI Deal Hunter helps one person research, verify and track deals, promotions and money-making offers for their own use. It runs locally on the owner's Windows PC. It is not a hosted service, and it is not available to the public or to other users.

How it uses Gmail

  • It connects only to the owner's own Gmail account, with the owner's consent.
  • It requests one permission: gmail.readonly (view messages). It cannot send, delete, label, archive or modify email.
  • About every 12 hours it reads only messages that match offer-related searches (for example promotions, bank and cashback offers). It skips security and sign-in emails.
  • The matching messages are stored in a local database on the owner's computer, where the owner reviews them.

Full details are in the privacy policy.

Who can use it

Only its owner. There are no sign-ups, accounts or customers. The Google sign-in exists solely so that the owner can authorize read-only access to their own mailbox.
